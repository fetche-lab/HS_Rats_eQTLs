## Suggested Next Steps
1. **CRISPR/qPCR validation** of Immediate Priority genes (shared strong cis-eQTLs).
2. **Allele-specific expression (ASE)** check from RNA-seq BAMs for top 10 genes.
3. **Colocalization** with metabolic trait GWAS if available (e.g., COLOC for Fam111a, Scd3, Lcn2).
4. **Gene set enrichment** on the full final_actionable_candidates.csv list using clusterProfiler/Enrichr.
5. **Fine-mapping** with SuSiE on the top 5 loci to identify causal variants.
6. **Trans-band follow-up**: For the top 3 cross-tissue trans-bands (Chr8:40-45 Mb, Chr5:160-165 Mb, Chr4:105-115 Mb), inspect raw read depth and founder haplotype effects to distinguish CNVs from mapping artifacts.
7. **Pangenomic re-mapping**: Propose mapping RNA-seq reads from trans-band loci against a rat pangenome graph. If signal collapses, it is a mapping artifact; if it persists, it tags a genuine structural variant acting as a master regulator.
